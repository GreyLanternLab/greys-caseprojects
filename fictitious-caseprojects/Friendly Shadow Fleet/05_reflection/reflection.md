# 🪞 Reflection
## 05_reflection

### Methodological Learning Point

This case study demonstrated that OSINT does not always lead to definitive conclusions.

The absence of data can itself guide the direction of a new research strategy.

---

### MARINT Development

During this case, the focus shifted from person-centric searching towards:

- systems thinking
- infrastructure analysis
- maritime pattern recognition

This marked a clear development within my OSINT learning journey.

---

### Ethics & Proportionality

I deliberately chose to:

- avoid compiling extensive lists of individual names
- refrain from analysing private profiles
- focus on processes rather than individuals

---

### What Worked Well

- Pivoting from a person-based approach to a route-based analysis
- Using Equasis to understand management structures
- Combining MARINT and GEOINT perspectives

---

### What I Would Do Differently

- Start earlier with infrastructure-focused analysis instead of person-centric OSINT
- Use more map layers during course analysis
- Incorporate historical AIS context at an earlier stage

---

### Personal Conclusion

This case confirmed that OSINT is not only about finding information, but about understanding systems.

The primary value of this research lay in the process and the analytical growth it enabled.
