# 🎯 Scope & Research Questions
## 01_scope

### Primary Objective

To conduct an exploratory OSINT/MARINT analysis of maritime routes originating from Zeebrugge towards the United Kingdom, based on a fictional background scenario.

---

### Research Questions

1. Which shipping companies operate routes from Zeebrugge towards the UK?
2. Which vessels are structurally active on these connections?
3. What do the sailing patterns look like?
4. Which infrastructure and terminals play a role?

Optional:

- Which roles and functions exist within this maritime context?

---

### Out of Scope

- Identification of real individuals
- Social engineering
- Paid data sources
- Dark web investigations

---

### Ethical Principles

- Use of open sources only
- Data minimisation
- No publication of identifiable personal data
- Analysis based on patterns, not accusations
