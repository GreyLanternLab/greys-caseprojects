# 🕵️ Case Study — The Friendly Shadow Fleet
## 00_context

### Research Context

This case study is part of a personal OSINT learning journey with a focus on maritime analysis (MARINT) within a fictional investigative journalism setting related to human trafficking.

The initial scenario and personal names are fictional.  
All information used in this case study originates from publicly accessible sources.

Objectives of this case:

- Developing structured methodologies using OSINT and MARINT
- Practicing infrastructure and route analysis
- Reflecting on proportionality and ethics in open-source investigations

This portfolio version contains no identifiable personal data.

---

### Fictional Background Scenario

During an informal gathering, a story was shared about possible stowaways on commercial shipping routes departing from Zeebrugge towards the United Kingdom.

The information was vague and not verifiable.  
Only one name was mentioned (referred to in this case study as **John Doe**).

Due to the lack of concrete leads, the research focus shifted towards:

- routes
- vessels
- shipping companies
- port infrastructure

---

### Research Objective

To collect and structure publicly accessible information about:

- maritime routes originating from Zeebrugge
- relevant shipping companies
- potential vessels
- operational maritime context

The emphasis of this study lies on the learning process rather than identifying individuals.
