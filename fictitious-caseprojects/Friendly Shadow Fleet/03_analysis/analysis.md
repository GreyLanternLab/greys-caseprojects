# 🔍 Collection — Data Gathering
## 02_collection

### Research Domains

The investigation was conducted across four OSINT domains:

- Open Web Search
- SOCMINT (learning purposes)
- Entities & Registries
- MARINT & GEOINT

---

### Open Web Search

Search queries focused on:

- maritime routes originating from Zeebrugge
- ferry and RoRo services towards the UK
- news articles related to smuggling contexts

Key outcome:

> The fictional individual mentioned in the background scenario had no verifiable online footprint.

This resulted in a pivot from person-centric research towards infrastructure-focused analysis.

---

### SOCMINT

SOCMINT was used primarily as a learning exercise:

- public company pages
- role descriptions within shipping companies
- general professional context
- exploring the potential to identify new sources among (former) crew members or personnel connected to the route/vessel (if required in a later phase)

No extensive dataset of individual names was intentionally created.
